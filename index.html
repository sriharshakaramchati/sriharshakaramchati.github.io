I’m Harsha. Co-founder of Reclaim Protocol (YC W21), where we build cryptographic infrastructure that lets anyone prove facts about their data without exposing it. This site is the index of everything I build: the company, the experiments, the writing, and the side quests.

AI didn’t break cryptography. It made everything else unverifiable. In a world where anything can be generated, only what can be proven matters.
