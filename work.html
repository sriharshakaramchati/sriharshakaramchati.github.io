reclaim protocol

co-founder, growth. reclaim lets apps verify claims about users with cryptographic proofs - no APIs, no passwords, no screenshots. prove “i graduated from this university” or “i earn this income” directly from your own logged-in session, and share only the proof. education and employment verification across 90+ countries and 29k+ universities, from $0.10 per verification.

popcorn

an attestable browser cloud, open-sourced: give your ai agent a browser that nobody can look inside - not the cloud provider, not us. agents running in an isolated enclave eventually hit a login wall. popcorn’s answer is the handoff: the human takes over the session for ten seconds, signs in, hands it back. the agent continues and never sees the password.

the verifiable-ai stack

verifiable inference proves the model computed exactly what it claims. it says nothing about where the input came from. correct compute on fake input is still fake. the end-to-end stack needs both halves: proof that the input arrived untampered from a real source, and proof that the compute ran honestly. i build the input side - and partner with the people proving the compute.
